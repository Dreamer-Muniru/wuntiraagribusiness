Wuntira Agri Foundation · WAF

Investing in people to grow a stronger agricultural future.

Founded in 2019, Wuntira Agri Foundation works to improve food security, food supply and local job opportunities through capacity-building programmes for financially disadvantaged communities.

Who we are

A future where young people and women can thrive in agriculture.

Our vision is to create sustainable youth employment across the agricultural value chain by fostering climate-smart agriculture and bio-circular business practices among youth and women. We train people in the field, stay engaged after training and work openly with communities about money and decisions.

4,352Youth, women smallholder farmers and aspiring agripreneurs trained
17Farmer and shea cooperatives currently supported

Programmes and services

Building skills, enterprise and resilience.

Climate-smart food production capacity building

Promoting sustainable food production that reduces environmental impact and improves soil health through demonstration farms, local and scientific knowledge transfer, tailored agronomic support and improved farming technologies.

Innovative agribusiness solutions

Training youth, rural farmers, green SMEs, agro-processors and aggregators in innovative technologies, agribusiness development and management, entrepreneurship, productivity and value-added processing.

Technical training in circular bioeconomy

Practical training from business ideation through branding and marketing, encouraging wealth creation from waste materials through sustainable enterprises and jobs.

Community support through cooperatives and charity

Supporting female youth through farmer-led cooperative formation, sexual and reproductive health rights education, gender equality advocacy and mobilising essential resources.

Financial support and market linkage

Connecting farmer and shea cooperatives to pre-financing facilities and local and international commodity buyers, while promoting fair trade, transparency, trust and accountability.

Cooperative governance and financial literacy

Building practical capacities in cooperative governance, entrepreneurship, financial literacy, market access and product development.
